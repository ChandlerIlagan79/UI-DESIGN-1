Admission Guide

From application to enrollment, step by step

Steps

  1. Check eligibility. Read the admission policies.
  2. Prepare documents. Gather every required file and photo.
  3. Apply online. Create an account in the admission portal and fill out the form.
  4. Take the exam. Attend the schedule sent to your email.
  5. Check results. Results are posted in the portal.
  6. Enroll. Submit original documents and complete registration.
